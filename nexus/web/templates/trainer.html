Map prep

About 10 minutes before a match on that map: one taps to warm up, then every spot of the map you can stand on (map angles), one round of your weakest skill, and at the end the callouts quiz of that map. The match-day page links here with the week's map.

The callout shows when one appears: call it. Only a headshot kills.
