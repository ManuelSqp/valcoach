DPI

Put a ruler next to your mouse. Click here, move the mouse exactly 10 cm to the right (straight, any speed), then click again.

    Three tries or more give a good average. Use the same edge of the mouse at the start and the end, on the mousepad you play on. Differences up to about 5% are normal for many sensors.

    Polling rate

    Click here, then move the mouse in fast circles for a few seconds.

    How many times a second the mouse reports to the PC (set in the mouse's software). Reliable up to 1000 Hz; for 2000–8000 Hz mice a browser shows less than the mouse sends.

    Monitor refresh rate

    Measuring…–

    The frames your screen really shows in this window. Lower than your monitor's rate? Check Windows › Display › Advanced display › refresh rate, the cable (DisplayPort or HDMI 2.1 for high rates), and that this window is on that monitor. The square should move smoothly without jumps.

    Buttons

    Click here with every button, one click at a time. A button that registers two clicks when you pressed once is worn.
