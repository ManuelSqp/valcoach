How it works

  1. One of you starts the evening and hosts it: he moves from round to round, everyone else follows on his own screen.
  2. For each round you see the buys, the kills, the plant and the minimap replay. Mark your own round, with a note if you want.
  3. Short on time? Key rounds only jumps through the rounds that decided the match.
  4. The host ends the evening: the summary goes into this week's practice plan, and the owner can post it to Discord.
